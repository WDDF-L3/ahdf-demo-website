International Cooperation

International cooperation

Connecting Bangladesh with international knowledge and capability.

The Company Profile describes a long-term cooperation platform that may connect Bangladesh with expertise, technology, education, skills development, healthcare, research and institutional learning.

Japan, the Republic of Korea and Italy are named as priority international directions. Future work depends on partner interest, country-specific requirements, signed arrangements and relevant regulatory approvals.

Ethical skills mobility

  • Skills recognition, language proficiency and cultural orientation
  • Technical training, career preparation and workplace conduct
  • Worker rights awareness, protection and returnee reintegration
  • Regulated recruitment only with separate licensing and authorization
Cooperation model

Four ways to work together.

Exchange

Expert exchange, language education, cultural orientation and training collaboration.

Research

Joint research, surveys, technical assistance and pilot programmes.

Skills

Competency-based TVET, curricula, workforce readiness and quality assurance.

Institutions

MoUs, grants, equipment support, CSR cooperation and approved partnerships.
