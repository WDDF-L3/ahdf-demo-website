Country-specific alignment

Updated cooperation narrative matched with the earlier page structure.

The existing country-based international cooperation layout has been aligned with the company profile’s partner-country direction and the broader international cooperation statement.

Bangladesh–Japan

A priority direction focused on skills and TVET, education, language, workplace culture, cross-cultural competency, healthcare, technology, climate action, youth exchange, research and institutional capacity building.

Bangladesh–Korea

A long-term cooperation platform intended to link Bangladesh with Korean expertise, innovation, training systems, research, technology transfer and broader development partnerships.

Bangladesh–Italy

A collaboration direction centred on education, language, healthcare, technology, research, cultural exchange, climate action and institutional strengthening through credible partner institutions.

Potential forms of partnership

  • Technical assistance
  • Curriculum development
  • Capacity building and training
  • Knowledge exchange and technology transfer
  • Research cooperation and equipment support
  • Exchange programmes and joint pilot projects
  • Grants, CSR partnerships and strategic cooperation agreements
  • Memoranda of Understanding (MoU)

Potential partner institutions

  • Embassies and diplomatic missions
  • Development agencies and UN entities
  • Universities and academic institutions
  • Hospitals and healthcare bodies
  • Philanthropic foundations and CSR units
  • Technology organizations and professional bodies
  • Other credible institutions aligned with approved programmes
