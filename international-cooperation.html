International Cooperation

International cooperation

A long-term platform for knowledge, skills and development partnerships.

AHDF seeks to connect Bangladesh with international expertise, knowledge, technology, training, institutional learning and development partnerships.

The Foundation’s profile identifies Bangladesh–Japan, Bangladesh–Korea and Bangladesh–Italy human development platforms as priority directions for potential cooperation. These are proposed areas of engagement, not a list of confirmed institutional agreements.

Three cooperation platforms

Bangladesh–Japan

Bangladesh–Korea

Bangladesh–Italy

Cooperation platform 01

Bangladesh–Japan Human Development Platform

Bangladesh–Japan cooperation is an important institutional focus within AHDF’s wider international framework. The intended platform would connect institutions with expertise, technology, training and development partnerships.

Skills & TVETKnowledge exchangeInstitutional learning

Potential areas of cooperation

  • Skills development and Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET)
  • Education, language training and workplace culture
  • Cross-cultural competency and cultural exchange
  • Healthcare and technology cooperation
  • Climate action, knowledge exchange and youth exchange
  • Research and institutional capacity building
Cooperation platform 02

Bangladesh–Korea Human Development Platform

Within AHDF’s intended international collaboration strategy, stronger institutional ties between Bangladesh and Korea are identified as a priority. The proposed platform would enable knowledge resources, technological innovation, capacity-building training and institutional learning.

TrainingTechnology transferResearch

Potential areas of cooperation

  • TVET, education and language training
  • Workplace culture orientation and cross-cultural competency building
  • Healthcare and technology transfer
  • Climate action and knowledge sharing
  • Youth and cultural exchange programmes
  • Research and institutional capacity strengthening
Cooperation platform 03

Bangladesh–Italy Human Development Platform

AHDF’s profile identifies Bangladesh–Italy institutional cooperation as another key direction within its long-term international platform. The intended approach would link Bangladesh with global expertise, technology, knowledge-sharing and broader development partnerships.

EducationHealthcareClimate action

Potential areas of cooperation

  • Technical and vocational skills development (TVET)
  • Education, language training and workplace culture orientation
  • Cross-cultural competency building
  • Healthcare, technology transfer and climate action
  • Knowledge sharing and youth and cultural exchange programmes
  • Research and institutional capacity strengthening
Partnership approach

Potential partners and cooperation arrangements.

The following institutional participants and arrangements are identified in the AHDF Company Profile as possibilities for international engagement.

Potential international partners

  • Embassies, diplomatic missions and development agencies
  • United Nations entities and academic institutions
  • Universities, hospitals and healthcare bodies
  • Philanthropic foundations and corporate social responsibility (CSR) departments
  • Technology organizations, firms, professional associations and other credible institutions

Possible cooperation modalities

  • Technical assistance, grants and funding support
  • Training, equipment provision and knowledge exchange
  • Research, exchange programmes and volunteer initiatives
  • Joint pilot projects and collaborative programmes
  • Memoranda of understanding, project agreements and strategic cooperation frameworks

Implementation and accountability

As outlined in the Company Profile, cooperation arrangements should define roles and responsibilities and be supported by approved workplans or implementation plans, budgets, reporting mechanisms and applicable compliance requirements. The platforms above describe potential international cooperation rather than confirmed partnerships.
