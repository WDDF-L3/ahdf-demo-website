About Us

Asia Human Development Foundation (AHDF)

AHDF is an independent, non-profit, non-political and public-benefit foundation focused on training, education, research, capacity building, charitable development and international cooperation.

Its stated objectives include human capital development, TVET, green skills, entrepreneurship, technology and AI, research, standards, quality assurance and ethical international skills mobility.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To empower people and institutions through education, capacity building, skills development, research, innovation and international cooperation.

Vision

To contribute to a more capable, inclusive and sustainable Asia where individuals and institutions can access learning, opportunity and meaningful development.
