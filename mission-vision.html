Mission & Vision

Mission

An inclusive, resilient and future-ready society.

To foster an inclusive, resilient and future-ready society where youth, women, persons with disabilities and underserved communities have equitable access to quality education, skills development, productive employment, entrepreneurship, innovation and global mobility opportunities, contributing to sustainable economic growth and shared prosperity.

Vision

Human capital and shared prosperity through responsible development.

To advance human capital development through demand-driven education and skills development, Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET), workforce development, entrepreneurship, ethical labour mobility, digital transformation, green economy initiatives, inclusive employment, sustainable livelihoods, community wellbeing and international cooperation at national, regional and global levels.

Content basis: The mission and vision above follow the AHDF Company Profile, page 5 (printed numbering). The organizational goal is to build a skilled, inclusive, innovative and globally competitive workforce through education, entrepreneurship, ethical migration, technology and sustainable livelihoods.
Core commitments

What guides the mission.

Capability

Market-relevant education, TVET, workforce readiness, career development and entrepreneurship.

Quality

Competency-based learning, quality assurance, occupational standards and measurable outcomes.

Exchange

Bangladesh-led international knowledge exchange, technical assistance and cultural cooperation.

Public benefit

Human dignity, equity, accessibility, safeguarding, green growth and community wellbeing.

Our guiding principles

Core Values and Operating Principles

Human Dignity and Rights

Respect dignity, rights and wellbeing and avoid discriminatory practice.

Equity, Diversity and Inclusion

Promote equal opportunity and meaningful participation for diverse groups.

Integrity and Accountability

Maintain ethical conduct, transparent stewardship and responsible decision making.

Sustainability and Climate Responsibility

Advance environmentally responsible and climate-resilient development.

Innovation and Excellence

Encourage practical innovation, learning, technology and quality improvement.

Partnership and Collaboration

Work with credible public, private, civil-society and international partners.

Transparency and Good Governance

Use needs assessment, monitoring, evidence and learning to improve programme quality.

Lifelong Learning and Continuous Improvement

Promote ongoing learning, reskilling, upskilling and organizational learning.

Safeguarding and Do No Harm

Prioritize safety, dignity, protection and responsible programme conduct.

Evidence and Results

Use needs assessment, monitoring, evidence and learning to improve programme quality.
