Categories of Member

4.1. Founder Member: Founder members of the Foundation shall be persons who are Board of Directors.

4.2. Regular Member: Government-licensed sending organizations and approved HR institutions supporting recruitment, screening and related processes.

4.3. General Members: Any individual who supports the objectives, mission and activities of the Foundation may apply to become a General Member.

4.4. Institutional Members: Educational institutions, training institutes, research organizations, nonprofit organizations, foundations, associations and other eligible institutions.

4.5. Corporate Members: Companies, business organizations, chambers, industry associations, social enterprises and other corporate entities.

4.6. Donors Members: Any individual, institution, organization, company or other entity making significant financial or in-kind contributions to the Foundation may be recognized as a Donor Member.

4.7. Honorary Members: The Board of Directors may confer Honorary Membership on distinguished persons from government, universities, research bodies and industry groups.

4.8. International Members: Individuals, professionals, experts, institutions, nonprofit organizations, universities, research organizations, development organizations and other eligible entities based outside Bangladesh.
