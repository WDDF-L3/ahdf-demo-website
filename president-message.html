President message

Sustainable development begins with people.

It is my privilege to present the institutional profile of Asia Human Development Foundation (AHDF), an organization established with a long-term commitment to human development, skills, inclusion, sustainable livelihoods and responsible international cooperation. AHDF is founded on the belief that sustainable development begins with people. Access to quality education, relevant skills, decent work, ethical labour mobility, entrepreneurship, technology, healthcare, environmental knowledge and social inclusion can enable individuals and communities to build resilient and dignified futures.

Our approach is intentionally integrated. We seek to connect education with employability, skills with enterprise, language with cultural understanding, labour mobility with worker protection, technology with inclusion, and climate action with green livelihoods. At the community level, we aim to combine social services and capacity building with measurable development outcomes. AHDF also recognizes the importance of credible partnerships. We therefore seek constructive cooperation with government institutions, development partners, educational and training institutions, employers, research organizations, civil society, private-sector actors, philanthropic organizations and international partners. Bangladesh–Japan cooperation is an important area of institutional interest, particularly in skills, education, health, technology, climate, language and cultural exchange.

We are committed to responsible governance, transparency, accountability, safeguarding, evidence-based programming and continuous institutional learning. Our ambition is not simply to deliver activities, but to build models that are useful, measurable, scalable and responsive to the needs of people and communities. I sincerely hope this profile will provide partners and stakeholders with a clear understanding of AHDF’s purpose, programme directions, governance framework and partnership potential.

Md. Rafiqul Islam (Pulok)
Founding President, AHDF

Source: “Message from the President”, AHDF Company Profile, page 1 (printed numbering). The full message is presented above in three paragraphs for the existing page layout.
