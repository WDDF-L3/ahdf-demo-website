Our areas of work

Detailed Programme and Areas of Work

AHDF may undertake programmes, projects, services and institutional partnerships in the following areas, subject to applicable law, approvals and operational capacity.

Programme area 01

Education and Capacity Development

AHDF may support equitable access to education and lifelong learning, particularly for disadvantaged and underserved learners.

  • Scholarships and educational support
  • School improvement initiatives
  • Digital learning and STEM awareness
  • Teacher and instructor capacity development
  • Modern teaching and learning methodologies
  • Learning materials and educational resources
  • Community learning initiatives
  • Digital literacy and lifelong learning
Programme area 02

Skills Development, TVET and Employment

AHDF will promote market-relevant technical, vocational and employability skills that respond to evolving workforce demand.

  • TVET and industry-oriented skills development
  • Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET)
  • Competency-based assessment and certification
  • Apprenticeship and Dual Training System (DTS)
  • Work-Based Learning and internships
  • Career counselling and employment preparation
  • Occupational safety and health awareness
  • Trainer and instructor development
  • Professional certification pathways
  • Reskilling and upskilling
  • Skills research and labour market assessments
Programme area 03

Language, Cultural and Workforce Readiness

AHDF will integrate language proficiency, cultural understanding, behaviour, ethics and workplace readiness into human capital development.

  • Foreign language education and proficiency development
  • Language assessment and examination preparation
  • Industry-specific language and communication
  • Cross-cultural competency and intercultural communication
  • Workplace behaviour and professional conduct
  • Professional ethics
  • International work culture orientation
  • Destination-specific workforce preparation
  • Customer service and professional communication
  • Daily-life and social adaptation orientation
  • International exchange and cultural cooperation
Programme area 04

Ethical Labour Mobility and Migration Governance

AHDF will support informed, rights-aware and ethically managed labour mobility pathways while respecting all applicable recruitment and migration regulations.

  • Ethical recruitment principles and fair employment practices
  • Ethical, safe, orderly and regular migration awareness
  • Labour mobility information and guidance
  • Skills recognition and certification
  • Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)
  • Pre-departure orientation and preparedness
  • Language and cultural preparation
  • Worker rights awareness and protection
  • Migrant welfare and referral support
  • Returnee migrant reintegration
  • Returnee entrepreneurship and livelihood support
  • Bilateral and multilateral labour cooperation
  • Japan-related pathways, including language, care-worker readiness and SSW readiness through authorized partners
Programme area 05

Entrepreneurship, Start-up and SME Development

AHDF will promote entrepreneurship as a pathway to employment, income generation, innovation and local economic resilience.

  • Entrepreneurship education
  • Business development training
  • Start-up incubation
  • SME development
  • Social enterprise development
  • Business mentoring
  • Financial literacy
  • Market-oriented enterprise skills
  • Technology-enabled enterprise
  • Green enterprise development
  • Women- and youth-led enterprise support
  • Returnee entrepreneur support
Programme area 06

Digital Transformation, Technology and Innovation

AHDF will support digital inclusion and future workforce development through technology-oriented skills, services and innovation initiatives.

  • ICT and digital literacy
  • Software development
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Data analytics
  • Cybersecurity
  • Cloud computing
  • Digital marketing
  • E-commerce
  • FinTech and digital financial services
  • Robotics and automation
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Emerging technologies
  • Online freelancing readiness
  • Technology entrepreneurship
  • Innovation labs and incubation
  • Smart workforce development
  • Responsible technology use
  • Beneficiary databases, dashboards and digital reporting
Programme area 07

Green Skills, Climate Resilience and Environmental Sustainability

AHDF will promote green skills, climate resilience and environmentally responsible livelihoods and technologies.

  • Green skills development
  • Green jobs promotion
  • Clean energy competencies
  • Renewable energy education and technology
  • Resource efficiency
  • Circular economy
  • Waste management
  • Sustainable agriculture
  • Climate-smart livelihoods
  • Environmental conservation
  • Water conservation
  • Tree plantation
  • Disaster preparedness
  • Climate adaptation and mitigation awareness
  • Carbon awareness
Programme area 08

Healthcare and Community Wellbeing

AHDF may implement community health-support initiatives with qualified professionals and partner institutions.

  • Medical camps
  • Maternal and child health awareness
  • Nutrition support and awareness
  • Preventive care
  • Telemedicine connections
  • Mental health awareness
  • Health education
  • Referral assistance

  • Health-related capacity development
  • Priority support for underserved communities, elderly people, women, children, persons with disabilities and disaster-affected populations
Programme area 09

Women, Youth and Disability-Inclusive Development

AHDF will mainstream gender, youth participation and disability inclusion across relevant programmes.

  • Women’s leadership and economic empowerment

  • Financial literacy and rights awareness

AHDF will support community-based development and may pilot an integrated village model combining social, economic and environmental interventions.

Knowledge exchange and partnerships

International Human Development Platforms

The profile identifies Bangladesh–Japan and Bangladesh–Korea cooperation as part of AHDF’s international programme direction.

International cooperation

International Cooperation and Bangladesh–Japan Human Development Platform

AHDF seeks to build a long-term international cooperation platform that connects Bangladesh with external expertise, knowledge, technology, training, institutional learning and development partnerships. Bangladesh–Japan cooperation is an important institutional focus within this broader international framework.

Potential areas of cooperation include skills and TVET, education, language, workplace culture, cross-cultural competency, health, technology, climate action, knowledge exchange, youth and cultural exchange, research and institutional capacity building.

Potential international partners may include embassies, development agencies, United Nations entities, universities, hospitals, philanthropic foundations, CSR departments, technology organizations, professional associations and other credible institutions.

Possible cooperation modalities include technical assistance, grants, training, equipment support, research, exchange programmes, volunteer initiatives, joint pilots, memoranda of understanding, project agreements and strategic cooperation frameworks. All such arrangements should be supported by clear roles, approved workplans, budgets, reporting and compliance requirements.

International cooperation

International Cooperation and Bangladesh–Korea Human Development Platform

AHDF is committed to establishing a sustainable, long-term platform for international collaboration—one that links Bangladesh with global expertise, knowledge resources, technological innovation, capacity-building training, institutional learning opportunities, and broader development partnerships. Within this wider international strategy, strengthening institutional ties between Bangladesh and Korea stands out as a key priority area.

The scope of potential collaboration spans multiple sectors, including technical and vocational skills development (TVET), education, language training, workplace culture orientation, cross-cultural competency building, healthcare, technology transfer, climate action initiatives, knowledge sharing, youth and cultural exchange programs, as well as research and institutional capacity strengthening.
