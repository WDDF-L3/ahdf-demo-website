Our Programmes

Programme architecture

Six connected programme domains.

These domains translate the foundation's aims into a clear public-facing programme structure. Actual projects can be added beneath each domain as they are approved and launched.

Human Capital & Employability

Workforce upskilling, leadership, career preparation, competency assessment and industry-recognized credential pathways.

Education, TVET & Language

Technical and vocational education, lifelong learning, digital literacy, language learning and instructor development.

Green Skills & Climate Resilience

Clean energy, circular economy, resource efficiency, environmental conservation and climate adaptation competencies.

Entrepreneurship & Inclusive Growth

SMEs, start-up incubation, social enterprise and opportunity creation for youth, women and underserved communities.

Technology, AI & Innovation

Responsible digital transformation, artificial intelligence, automation, applied research and innovation ecosystems.

Research, Standards & Policy

Research, surveys, pilots, evaluation, publications, quality assurance, occupational standards and accreditation readiness.

Cross-cutting theme: International cooperation

Each programme can include knowledge exchange, institutional partnerships, technology transfer and international benchmarking.

Cross-cutting theme: Ethics & quality

AHDF's objectives emphasize ethical skills mobility, quality assurance, standards, assessment and lawful operation.
