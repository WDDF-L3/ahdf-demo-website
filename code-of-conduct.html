Code of Conduct

Draft public code

Safeguarding, integrity and responsible participation.

The AHDF Company Profile sets out commitments to human dignity, non-discrimination, safeguarding, transparency, partner due diligence, confidential feedback and responsible use of personal information.

Act lawfully and ethically

Observe applicable law and obtain required permits or authorizations before regulated work.

Protect people from unfair practices

Protect people from exploitation, abuse, harassment and discrimination; support safe participation.

Disclose conflicts of interest

Disclose conflicts of interest and follow fair, transparent procurement and decision-making practices.

Respect confidentiality and data

Respect confidentiality, personal information, photographic consent and authorized use of AHDF materials.

Support quality and accountability

Maintain accurate records, cooperate with appropriate reviews and use monitoring evidence for learning.

Approval required: The statements summarize principles in the Company Profile. They are not a substitute for any subsequently approved code of conduct, safeguarding policy or disciplinary procedure.
