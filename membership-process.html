Membership Process

A clear application, review and approval pathway for individuals, institutions, corporate bodies, donors and international members.

Become a Member
How to join

A Board-approved membership process.

The Rules provide a written application and approval process. The exact fees for most categories may be determined by the Board from time to time.

1

Choose your membership category

Select the category that best matches your role or organization: Founder, Regular, General, Institutional, Corporate, Donor, Honorary or International.

2

Complete the membership application

Provide your profile, contact information, relevant organization details and, where available, proposer/referee information.

3

Submit for AHDF review

The application is reviewed internally and membership remains subject to approval by the Board of Directors.

4

Approval, activation and membership ID

Approved applications can be activated after applicable requirements are completed and AHDF records the assigned membership ID.
